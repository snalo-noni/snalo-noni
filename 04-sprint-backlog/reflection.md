# Sprint Backlog Reflection

[← Back to portfolio](../README.md)

## Situation

The sprint goal was to publish the Soluon website with its core sections, a working contact form and the team section, ready for the demo on 16 October. I was working on it alongside school projects and a hackathon.

## Task

I was the only developer, so every sprint task was mine.

## Action

With no board, I tracked and reported progress at the Monday, Wednesday and Friday stand-ups and through WhatsApp updates. Limited time because of school projects and the hackathon was the main pressure. Deployment also had to wait, because the company had no domain and the Product Owner was unavailable on the day of the demo.

## Result

My plan was about right: the website was built in about three days and shown at the demo. I left too little buffer for the contact form and deployment, so next sprint I would leave more room for them.
